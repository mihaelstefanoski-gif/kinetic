Potential Energy (PE)

PE = m × g × h

Stored energy based on height! The higher you lift the ball above the ground (h) or the heavier it is (m), the more gravitational potential energy it stores.

🚀

Kinetic Energy (KE)

KE = ½ × m × v²

Energy of motion! As the ball rolls down the hill, stored potential energy converts directly into speed (v), reaching maximum kinetic energy at the lowest dip.
